# Admission Brochure 2026

--- page 1 ---

## 1. Eligibility

For undergraduate programmes, a candidate must have passed the higher secondary
examination with at least 50 percent marks in aggregate. Candidates from a
reserved category need 45 percent.

For postgraduate programmes, a candidate must hold a bachelor's degree in a
relevant discipline with at least 55 percent marks. A candidate awaiting the
final semester result may apply provisionally.

## 2. Entrance test

The entrance test is held on 18 June 2026 and is two hours long. It carries 100
marks and has no negative marking. The test covers reasoning, general awareness,
and the candidate's chosen subject.

The admit card for the entrance test is released on 10 June 2026. A candidate
must carry the admit card and one photo identity document to the test centre.

## 3. Documents required

| Document | Requirement |
| Marksheet | Higher secondary marksheet, original and one copy |
| Transfer certificate | Original, issued by the last institution attended |
| Migration certificate | Required for candidates from another board or university |
| Category certificate | Required only for candidates claiming a reserved seat |
| Photographs | Four recent passport-size photographs |

An application missing any of the documents listed above is kept pending until
the document is produced. A pending application is not considered for the merit
list.

## 4. Important dates

| Event | Date |
| Application form opens | 1 May 2026 |
| Application form closes | 5 June 2026 |
| Entrance test | 18 June 2026 |
| Merit list published | 30 June 2026 |
| Fee payment closes | 15 July 2026 |

## 5. Fee structure

The annual tuition fee for undergraduate programmes is 24,000 rupees. For
postgraduate programmes it is 30,000 rupees. The admission fee of 2,000 rupees
is a one-time charge paid with the first year's tuition fee.

A candidate whose name appears on the merit list must pay the fee by 15 July
2026. A seat not confirmed by that date is offered to the next candidate on the
waitlist.

## 6. Reservation

Twenty-two percent of seats are reserved for candidates from the Scheduled
Castes and Scheduled Tribes, and twenty-seven percent for candidates from the
Other Backward Classes. Five percent of seats are reserved for candidates with a
disability.

A candidate claiming a reserved seat must produce a category certificate in the
prescribed format. A certificate in any other format is not accepted.

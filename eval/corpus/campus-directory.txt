## 3. Library

The central library is open from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to
17:00 on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

A student may borrow up to four books at a time for fourteen days. A book
returned late is charged two rupees per day. Reference books and journals may
not be borrowed.

The reading hall on the first floor stays open until 22:00 during the
examination months of December and April.

## 4. Hostel

The hostel warden's office is on the ground floor of Hostel Block H1 and is open
from 09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. The warden's extension is 1101.

Hostel gates close at 22:30. A student who needs to stay out later must inform
the warden in writing before 18:00 on the same day.

## 5. Canteen

The canteen is on the ground floor of the Student Centre and serves from 07:30 to
18:00. It accepts the campus card and cash. The canteen is closed on Sundays.

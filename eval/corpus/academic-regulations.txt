# Academic Regulations

--- page 1 ---

## 1. Course registration

Course registration for the odd semester opens on 1 August 2026 and closes on 20
August 2026. A student must register for a minimum of 18 credits and may register
for a maximum of 26 credits in a semester.

Registration is done on the student portal. A student who has not registered by
20 August 2026 is charged a late registration fee of 300 rupees and may register
only until 25 August 2026.

## 2. Attendance

A student must attend at least 75 percent of the classes held in each subject to
be eligible for the end-semester examination. Attendance is counted subject by
subject, not in aggregate.

A student whose attendance falls between 65 and 75 percent may apply for
condonation with a medical certificate. Condonation is granted by the Dean and
is not automatic. Attendance below 65 percent cannot be condoned.

--- page 2 ---

## 3. Credits and promotion

A semester carries 20 credits on average. A student needs a cumulative grade
point average of at least 5.0 to be promoted to the next academic year.

A student who fails more than two papers in a semester is not promoted and must
repeat the semester. A student who fails one or two papers is promoted
provisionally and clears the papers through the supplementary examination.

## 4. Bonafide certificate

A bonafide certificate is issued by the Academic Section on request. A student
must apply through the portal and collect the certificate from the Academic
Section counter after three working days.

The certificate confirms that the student is currently enrolled. It is not a
conduct certificate, and it does not state a student's marks.

--- page 3 ---

## 5. Transcripts and marksheets

A consolidated transcript is issued after the final semester result is published.
An application for a transcript costs 1,000 rupees and takes ten working days.

A duplicate marksheet is issued when the original is lost. The student must file
a written declaration and pay 400 rupees. A duplicate marksheet carries the same
serial number as the original.

## 6. Scholarship

A merit scholarship is awarded to the top two students of each programme each
year. The scholarship covers half the tuition fee for the following year.

A student applying for a scholarship must submit the application to the
Academic Section by 31 August 2026. Applications received after that date are
considered in the next cycle.

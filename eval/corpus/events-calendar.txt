# Events Calendar 2026-27

--- page 1 ---

## 1. Orientation

Orientation for the incoming batch is held on 25 July 2026 in the Dr. Kalam
Auditorium. Attendance is compulsory for all first-year students. Parents may
attend the morning session.

The orientation covers the academic regulations, the campus facilities, and the
anti-ragging policy. Identity cards are issued at the end of the session.

## 2. Cultural fest

The annual cultural fest, Aaroh, is held from 14 to 16 September 2026. It opens
with an inter-college quiz on the first evening and closes with a music concert.

Registration for events opens on 1 September 2026. A student may register for up
to three events. The registration fee is 100 rupees per event.

--- page 2 ---

## 3. Sports meet

The annual sports meet is held from 5 to 8 November 2026 at the main ground.
Track events are held in the morning and team games in the afternoon.

A student who wishes to take part must submit a fitness declaration signed by a
doctor. The declaration must reach the Physical Education department by 25
October 2026.

## 4. Annual day

Annual day is celebrated on 20 February 2027 in the Dr. Kalam Auditorium.
Academic prizes and the merit scholarship certificates are presented on that
day.

## 5. Holidays

| Holiday | Date |
| Republic Day | 26 January 2027 |
| Holi | 3 March 2027 |
| Independence Day | 15 August 2026 |
| Gandhi Jayanti | 2 October 2026 |
| Founder's Day | 12 December 2026 |

The college remains closed on the holidays listed above. The hostel and the
canteen remain open on all holidays except Founder's Day.

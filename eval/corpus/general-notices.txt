# General Notices

--- page 1 ---

## 1. College address and timings

The college is at 14 College Road, Vidyanagar, Pune 411007. The main gate is open
from 07:00 to 21:00. The administrative offices work from 09:30 to 17:30 on
weekdays and are closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

Classes run from 08:30 to 16:30 on weekdays. A student who arrives after the
first period is marked absent for that period.

## 2. Notice board

The official notice board is in the corridor outside the Administrative Block,
next to the Examination Cell. Notices are also published on the student portal.

A notice is treated as served on the day it is put up on the board. Students are
expected to check the board at least twice a week.

--- page 2 ---

## 3. Identity cards

Every student is issued an identity card at orientation. The card is required to
enter the campus, the library, and the examination hall.

A lost identity card is replaced by the Academic Section on payment of 200
rupees. A replacement takes three working days and requires a written complaint
and one photograph.

## 4. Lost and found

Articles found on campus are kept at the security desk at the main gate. An
article that is not claimed within 30 days is disposed of by the college.

## 5. Anti-ragging

Ragging in any form is prohibited on campus and in the hostel. A complaint may
be made to the Anti-Ragging Committee through the drop box at the main gate or
through the portal.

A student found to have taken part in ragging is suspended and a police
complaint is filed. The identity of a complainant is kept confidential.
